# Lexicon — Vocabulary that sticks

Lexicon helps you learn advanced English vocabulary in a way that actually stays with you. Discover a new word every day, study it deeply with real context, write your own sentences, and review with a continuous spaced-repetition flow.

No account is required to try the demo. Sign in later to keep your progress across devices.

---

## What you can do

### Word of the Day
Every day features one carefully chosen advanced word. See its pronunciation, part of speech, definition, level, and category at a glance. Learn it, listen to it, or save it to your list in one click.

### Learn deeply
Open any word and get:
- Phonetic pronunciation + audio
- Clear definition and difficulty level
- Synonyms and antonyms
- Real example sentences
- Common collocations
- A writing box where you create your own sentence (the strongest way to make a word stick)

Mark words you already know so they stop cluttering your reviews.

### Smart review
A continuous review session that asks “How well do you know this word?” Rate each card:
- Don’t know → review again soon  
- Kind of know → review in a couple of days  
- Know it → longer interval  
- Mastered → it leaves the active queue  

Keyboard shortcuts keep the flow fast: Space to reveal, 1–4 to rate.

### My Words
Your personal vocabulary list with search and filters (All / Learning / Mastered). Study or mark any word as known from here.

### Progress dashboard
Track:
- Day streak
- Words known
- Words due for review
- XP and level
- Vocabulary breakdown by topic
- Recent activity and a short insight about your progress

Anonymous users see local progress; signed-in users get everything saved to their account.

### Capture (coming soon)
Paste a word or sentence from anything you’re reading and keep the original context. Scene mode will generate a mini vocabulary set tailored to a situation you describe.

### Browser extension (coming soon)
Hover any word on any website for a quick definition and the option to save it straight into your review list.

## Newsletter
Subscribe (free) to receive the Word of the Day by email every morning. Confirmation is required so only real addresses are added. You can unsubscribe anytime — from the site or from any email.

---

## Accounts
Create an account with just an email and password (no arbitrary character restrictions). Your local progress is synced automatically when you sign up or sign in. Sign out whenever you like.
